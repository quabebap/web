Remote & Cast: Smart TV · Last updated: July 31, 2026

Need help with the app? Most problems come down to the phone and the TV not being on the same Wi‑Fi network. Start with the checklist below — if that doesn’t solve it, email us and we’ll help.

Before anything else

  • Your TV and your iPhone/iPad must be on the same Wi‑Fi network (a 2.4 GHz and a 5 GHz band on the same router usually works, but guest networks and network isolation do not).
  • Your TV must be turned on and not in a deep standby mode.
  • Allow the Local Network permission when iOS asks. You can check it in the app under Settings → Diagnostics → Local Network Permission.
  • Turn off any VPN on your phone — a VPN routes traffic away from your local network and hides your TV.

Frequently asked questions

The app can’t find my TV

Work through these in order:

  • Confirm both devices are on the same Wi‑Fi network (not a guest network).
  • Disable any VPN or private‑relay feature on your phone.
  • Open Settings → Diagnostics in the app and tap Scan Again.
  • Restart the TV, then the app.
  • Some routers have “AP isolation” or “client isolation” enabled, which blocks devices from seeing each other. Turn it off in your router settings.
Which TVs are supported?

The app works with:

  • Android TV and Google TV
  • Samsung Smart TV
  • LG (webOS)
  • Sony (Bravia)
  • Chromecast and AirPlay‑compatible devices

Older or region‑specific models sometimes use a different protocol and may not be detected. If your TV is on the list but isn’t found, email us the exact model number.

Pairing asks for a code, but no code appears on my TV

On Android TV and Google TV, the TV shows a 6‑digit code the first time you pair. If nothing appears, tap Didn’t show code in the app to request it again, and make sure the TV is on its home screen rather than inside another app.

Why can’t I cast from YouTube, Netflix or Disney+?

Those services protect their video with DRM, and protected streams cannot be sent to another device by a third‑party app. This is a restriction imposed by the streaming services, not a limitation we can remove. Cast from the web works with pages that use a standard, unprotected video file.

Screen mirroring doesn’t show up on my TV

Screen mirroring works by opening a web address in your TV’s browser. Make sure your TV has a browser app, and that both devices are on the same Wi‑Fi network. If the TV doesn’t open the page automatically, type the address shown in the app into the TV’s browser manually.

I bought Premium but still see ads

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account that made the original purchase. Premium is a one‑time purchase, not a subscription, and it stays with your Apple Account.

How do I remove a paired TV?

Go to Settings, find the TV under Paired Television, and tap the trash icon on the right of the row. You’ll be asked to confirm.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through them. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, and choose the purchase you’d like refunded.

Does the app collect my data?

There are no user accounts, and the app never asks for your name, email or password. Photos, videos and voice input are handled on your device and sent only to your own TV over your local network.
