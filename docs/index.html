Your phone is now the only remote you need.

Control your smart TV, type without the on‑screen keyboard, and send photos, videos and your entire screen to the big screen — from your iPhone or iPad.

No account needed No extra hardware Works over Wi‑Fi

What it does

A real remote

Power, volume, mute and channels, plus a gesture trackpad, D‑pad, arrow keys and a number pad.

Type & talk

Search on your TV using your phone keyboard, or speak and have your words turned into text instantly.

Cast your media

Send photos and videos straight from your library to the TV, with AirPlay and Chromecast supported out of the box.

Cast from the web

Open a web page in the app, play a video, and send it to your TV.

Screen mirroring

Mirror your whole screen to any TV that has a browser — not just AirPlay devices.

Your TV’s apps

Open the apps installed on your television directly from your phone.

Works with

  • Android TV & Google TV
  • Samsung Smart TV
  • LG (webOS)
  • Sony (Bravia)
  • Chromecast
  • AirPlay devices

Your TV and your phone need to be on the same Wi‑Fi network. Some streaming services protect their video with DRM, and those protected streams can’t be cast by any third‑party app.
