Privacy Policy

tvremote · Last updated: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the tvremote mobile application (the “App”) handles information. The App is a universal remote and casting tool that helps you control and send media to televisions on your local Wi‑Fi network.

Plain-language summary. tvremote has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, or password. It uses third‑party services (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, and Apple) that collect limited device and usage data to run analytics, report crashes, show ads, and process purchases. Photos, videos, and voice you use in the App are handled on your device and sent only to your own TV over your local network — we never receive or store them on our servers.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal details. The only content you actively supply is the media you choose to cast:

  • Photos and videos you select from your photo library to display on your TV. These are processed on your device and served directly to your television over your local Wi‑Fi network. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Voice input you speak when using the microphone/search feature (see section 2.3).

2.2 Information collected automatically

Through the third‑party services below, the App and its providers may automatically collect:

  • Device & usage data — device model, operating system version, app version, language/region, and in‑app events and interactions (via Firebase Analytics).
  • Diagnostic & crash data — crash logs, stack traces, and device state at the time of a crash (via Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Advertising data — advertising and device identifiers, coarse location inferred from IP, and ad interaction/measurement data used to serve and measure ads (via Google AdMob).

2.3 Device permissions

The App asks for the following permissions only when you use the related feature. You can change these at any time in the iOS Settings app:

PermissionWhy the App uses it
Local NetworkTo discover, pair with, and control compatible TVs and to stream your chosen media to them over your Wi‑Fi network. This traffic stays on your local network.
Photo LibrarySo you can pick photos and videos to cast to your TV. Selection happens through Apple’s system picker.
MicrophoneTo capture your voice for the voice‑input feature.
Speech RecognitionTo convert your spoken words to text, which is sent to the TV’s on‑screen field. Speech recognition may be processed by Apple; see Apple’s privacy policy.

3. How we use information

  • To operate core features — discovering TVs, sending remote commands, casting media, mirroring, and voice/text entry.
  • To understand how the App is used and improve stability and features (analytics and crash reporting).
  • To display advertisements and to offer an ad‑free experience via in‑app purchase.
  • To process and validate the “Remove Ads” purchase through Apple.

4. Advertising

The App shows banner and full‑screen (interstitial) ads through Google AdMob unless you purchase “Remove Ads.” AdMob may use device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads and to limit how often you see the same ad.

The App does not currently present Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising. As a result, ads are served on a limited (non‑personalized) basis. You can further limit ad tracking in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and → Apple Advertising.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to measure usage and diagnose crashes. This helps us fix bugs and prioritize improvements. Data is processed by Google under Google’s privacy terms.

6. In‑app purchases

The App offers a one‑time “Remove Ads” purchase processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details; Apple handles the transaction and shares only limited purchase confirmation data with the App so it can unlock the ad‑free experience and restore prior purchases.

7. Third‑party services

The App relies on the following third parties, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Cast (Chromecast) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple (App Store, StoreKit, Speech Recognition) — apple.com/legal/privacy

8. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the third‑party service providers listed above so they can perform their functions (analytics, crash reporting, advertising, and payment processing), and where required to comply with the law or protect rights and safety.

9. Data retention

Media, voice input, and TV pairing details are kept only as long as needed to perform the action you requested and are stored locally on your device (pairing credentials are held in the iOS Keychain). Analytics, crash, and advertising data are retained by the respective providers according to their own retention policies.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information, including storing TV pairing secrets in the iOS Keychain and communicating with your TV over your local network. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, and to opt out of certain data uses. You can:

  • Purchase “Remove Ads” to stop advertising in the App.
  • Limit ad tracking and reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.
  • Revoke Local Network, Photos, Microphone, or Speech permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Contact us at quabebap1987@gmail.com to exercise applicable data rights.

13. International users
